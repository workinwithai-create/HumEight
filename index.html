HumEight

The melody is in your head; the DAW is in the way. Type your BPM and 4 chords, put on headphones, and hum 8 bars over a real band (FluidR3 piano, upright bass, strings, CC0 kit). HumEight pitch-tracks the take in your browser, snaps it to your key and grid, and plays it back on a real flute or grand piano. Export WAV + a multi-track MIDI where your line is its own track, on the grid from bar 1. No upload, no AI vocals, nothing leaves your machine.
